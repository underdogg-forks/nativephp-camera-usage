# 🟧 Rubik's Cube Scanning Guide

A step-by-step guide for scanning all 6 faces of your Rubik's Cube using the app.

---

## Step 1 — ⬜ Scan White Face (Top)

**Action:** Lay the cube flat on a table.

## Step 2 — 🟩 Scan Green Face (Front)

**Action:** Stand the cube upright.

*[Insert Photo Here: Show the cube standing upright with White on top and Green facing the camera]*


- ⬜ White on **TOP**
- 🟩 Green center **FACING YOU**

## Step 3 — 🟥 Scan Red Face (Right)

**Action:** Keeping White on top, rotate the cube **90° to the LEFT**.

- ⬜ White still on **TOP**
- 🟥 Red center now **FACING YOU**

Tap **Right** on the grid.

## Step 4 — 🟦 Scan Blue Face (Back)

**Action:** Keeping White on top, rotate the cube another **90° to the LEFT**.

*[Insert Photo Here: Show the cube upright with White on top and Blue facing the camera]*


- ⬜ White still on **TOP**
- 🟦 Blue center now **FACING YOU**

Tap **Back** on the grid.

---

## Step 5 — 🟧 Scan Orange Face (Left)

**Action:** Keeping White on top, rotate the cube another **90° to the LEFT**.

*[Insert Photo Here: Show the cube upright with White on top and Orange facing the camera]*


- ⬜ White still on **TOP**
- 🟧 Orange center now **FACING YOU**

Tap **Left** on the grid.

## Step 6 — 🟨 Scan Yellow Face (Bottom)

**Action:** From the Orange step, lay the cube flat again.

*[Insert Photo Here: Show the cube lying flat with Yellow on top and Green facing away]*


- ⬜ White face **DOWN**
- 🟩 Green facing **AWAY** from you
- 🟨 Yellow face **UP**

Point your camera **straight down** at the Yellow face.  
Tap **Bottom** on the grid.
